Undercover Auditor

Kaggle x Google AI Agents Intensive CapstoneJul 2026

A three-agent pipeline that audits public-facing FAQ answers against internal policy documents, flagging discrepancies before they reach customers.

Demo media coming soon

Overview

Undercover Auditor is a multi-agent compliance system built for the Kaggle x Google AI Agents Intensive capstone. It's designed to catch the gap between what a public FAQ tells customers and what a company's internal policy documents actually say — a mismatch that's easy to miss manually as policies evolve.

The system runs three agents in sequence: one extracts claims from the public FAQ, one retrieves the relevant internal policy sections, and one compares the two and flags discrepancies with a citation back to the source policy line. Every flag is fully traceable, so a human reviewer can verify it in seconds rather than re-reading the whole document.

Engineering Decisions

  • Built on Google ADK for agent orchestration. Structures the extract → retrieve → compare pipeline as discrete, testable agent roles rather than one large prompt.
  • Used FastMCP over a stdio subprocess. Keeps each agent's tool access least-privilege and isolated, so the policy-retrieval agent can't accidentally take actions outside its scope.
  • Chose keyword-scoring search over semantic retrieval. Semantic search would surface "close enough" matches, but for a compliance tool every flagged discrepancy needs to be traceable to an exact policy line — keyword scoring keeps that audit trail intact.
